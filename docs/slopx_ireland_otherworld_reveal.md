# Ireland Otherworld Archive Reveal

The Otherworld route crosses from plausible investigation into deliberate
absurdity through `slopx_ireland_events.437`, titled **The Archive Records What
Has Not Yet Happened**. The event is deliberately separate from the earlier
folklore and scholars flavour chain. It is the named H/P/A boundary required
by the Ireland specifications.

## Admission

The country must have the hidden-content permission and enabled game rule, the
committed Otherworld investigation, verified Otherworld evidence, independent
witnesses, the completed evidence ledger, an unusual campaign condition, and a
completed Hidden Evidence Council authorized by
`slopx_ireland_concealed_transition_authority`. The event is opened by the
exact-scoped Ireland hidden-permission refresh and cannot recur after its
durable reveal flag is recorded.

Before the event, the committed investigation remains P. The AI archive
opening is H, and the serious-culture/archive lane remains H until a route is
committed. No impossible flag is derived from merely completing the compact or
containment prerequisites.

## Player choices

Every option records the impossible occurrence and the route-specific
verified-impossibility boundary through
`slopx_ireland_commit_otherworld_archive_reveal`. The choices
then remain distinct:

- recognise contact and seek a compact, increasing public knowledge and
  consent while opening the compact lane;
- seal the evidence and contain the sites, lowering public knowledge and
  consent while improving the neutrality ledger;
- exploit contact for state power, increasing public knowledge and foreign
  exposure while damaging consent and recording the exploitation pressure;
- reject the interpretation and continue human investigation, preserving a
  human-restoration memory without returning the campaign to H. This choice
  opens the shared `Recovery Bridge` and then the `Restore Constitutional
  Calendar` focus. The bridge and calendar are a real recovery sequence: the
  review flags are transient and cleaned on route closure, while the completed
  restoration record remains durable. The route profile stays Otherworld A;
  human restoration changes who governs the contact, not whether the verified
  impossible event occurred.

Settlement movement scales from the live Social Consent ledger. All affected
values are clamped and recomputed in the event cleanup. The four temporary
option flags and the event-open flags are cleared in `after`, while the choice
memory and route branch flags remain durable for AI, conclusions, and audits.

## Assets and UI

No new image is required. The event reuses the cleared runtime picture
`GFX_report_event_ireland_report_otherworld_verification`, registered in
`interface/slopx_ireland_event_pictures.gfx` and stored under the runtime
`gfx/event_pictures/ireland/` root. No final DDS is read from `assets/`.

The compact and containment focuses additionally require the event-owned
`slopx_ireland_otherworld_archive_reveal_complete` flag, so the reveal event is
visible in the route's requirements rather than hidden behind the shared
High-King/Five-Provinces `slopx_ireland_verified_impossible_reveal` flag. That
shared flag remains available for the other hidden families and final
convergence, but it cannot bypass the Otherworld event.

## Future extension

The four durable choice flags can feed later compact-obligation, containment,
dominion, and human-restoration crises without changing the boundary event.
Any extension must preserve the rule that impossible authority cannot waive
human supply, law, rights, Northern settlement, or public legitimacy. The
human-restoration sequence must continue to use the ordinary emergency review,
constitutional calendar, and material conclusion gates; it cannot become a
free return to a historical profile.
