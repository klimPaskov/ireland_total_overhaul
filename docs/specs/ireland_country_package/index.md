# Ireland Country Package — Canonical Specification Index

Feature slug: `ireland_country_package`

Status: implementation source of truth for the 2026-08-14 campaign-depth pass.

## Purpose

This package reconciles the existing Ireland focus, Parliament, provision,
readiness, neutrality, Northern, territorial, industrial, cultural, event, and
advisor systems into one campaign architecture. It supersedes completion claims
that treat focus count, flag output, or the existence of a detached decision
category as proof of gameplay depth.

The existing `docs/specs/ireland_focus_tree/` package remains authoritative for
historical research and detailed subsystem contracts unless this package names a
conflict. This package has final authority over campaign navigation, route
identity, territorial expansion visibility, concrete payoff standards, route
conclusions, progressive disclosure, and implementation order.

## Player promise

Ireland begins with three understandable national problems:

1. establish a workable constitutional government;
2. rebuild a weak agricultural and infrastructural economy;
3. create a credible defence and neutrality policy.

The opening does not expose a wall of ideology. The National Settlement then
reveals five public political families: democratic republican government,
Congress socialism, Blueshirt authoritarianism, producer-estate oligarchy, and
constitutional restoration. Each family changes advisors, companies, laws,
security, economic institutions, military doctrine, diplomacy, Northern policy,
territorial ambition, and the postwar settlement.

Territorial policy is a connected campaign, not a floating far-right island.
Every family sees the national question after the Settlement, understands its
prerequisites from connectors and tooltips, selects a route-specific method, and
then receives active operations with equipment, access, intelligence, readiness,
state-control, and diplomatic requirements. Democratic Ireland pursues consent
and guarantees; socialist Ireland supports liberation; oligarchic Ireland seeks
commercial access and protectorates; fascist and restoration governments may
escalate to claims and war only after material preparation and foreign refusal.

## Non-negotiable architecture

- Exactly three visible opening trunks. Hidden and ideological families reveal
  later and never masquerade as opening roots.
- Five public political families appear as one readable post-Settlement choice
  band. Downstream profile choices remain separate from family commitment.
- Ordinary industry, army, air, navy, diplomacy, culture, Northern policy, and
  territorial policy are recognizable lanes or overlays. Naval content does not
  sit in the visual centre of unrelated politics.
- Every revealed focus is reachable or visibly explains the unmet requirement.
- A focus card cannot be justified only by setting an unconsumed flag, changing
  an invisible ledger, or preparing a later helper. It must grant a tangible
  effect, unlock a named playable action/system, transform a persistent
  institution, or make an immediately legible route commitment.
- Focus tooltips state the named outcome and important material effect. Filters
  describe direct effects, not thematic association.
- Important decisions are operations, not political-power purchases. Political
  power may represent administrative attention but cannot be the sole meaningful
  input for military, industrial, diplomatic, or territorial action.
- Event choices state exact visible value changes or exact fixed outcomes and
  identify what opens, closes, succeeds, or fails.
- Ordinary event cadence is paced. Reusable reaction chains use explicit
  brokers, cooldowns, and cleanup rather than daily universal dispatch.
- No automatic external cores, free foreign annexation, free full armies, or
  instant ideology transformations.
- Historical AI preserves constitutional neutrality and the real wartime/postwar
  trajectory. Alternate families require explicit ahistorical selection;
  extremist and impossible routes require dedicated permission.

## Completion rule

The package is complete only when the source implementation, current specs, AI,
localisation, icons, report art, advisor/company assets, decision and event
lifecycles, focus render, GUI states, and route-coverage audit agree. A working
loader or a large focus count is not sufficient.
