## Parliament lifecycle

All four buttons are visible from initialization. The current action uses a
green/brass enabled state; unavailable future actions are engine-darkened. Each
tooltip names the exact enabling focus, stage, majority, and PP requirement.

1. Convene Dáil: free opening institutional action when Dublin and valid stage are
   present; records the Settlement and reveals public architecture.
2. Win Seanad Support: requires National Settlement and 25 PP.
3. Confidence Pact: requires Constitutional Draft and 25 PP.
4. Joint Budget: requires Government Formation and 35 PP.

Native decisions mirror the same shared executors. No GUI route can bypass costs,
stage, or focus gates. The category contains a visible opening Dáil decision so
the category is not empty before the GUI opens, then progressively exposes later
Parliament decisions or transfers human interaction to the GUI without duplicates.

## Decision and mission rules

- A decision tooltip states requirements, duration, consumed resources, outcome,
  cancellation, and failure.
- Long-running work uses a mission or timed decision with one terminal cleanup
  path for success, timeout, cancellation, route change, and invalid scope.
- Equipment, fuel, trains, convoys, manpower, factories, command power, XP,
  intelligence, access, and state control are used where the action represents
  material work.
- PP alone is appropriate only for parliamentary administration, legal process,
  or diplomatic attention.
- Mission capacity is centralized. Starting, completing, cancelling, or timing
  out a mission recomputes capacity.

### Exceptional route lifecycle promotion

The High Kingship, Five Provinces, and Otherworld corridors share one
country-scoped value contract. Their focus completions call
`ireland_refresh_exceptional_route_state`, which initializes missing ledgers,
recomputes provincial mandates, synchronizes the active route profile and
spirits, and commits only the conclusion represented by a completed focus.
Terminal focuses do not manufacture component flags: pair and final
conclusions read their completed-focus history and exact scripted triggers.

The new focus effects fire the bounded event range `ireland_events.440` through
`ireland_events.460` where the route requires a player decision. Decisions and
missions own the timed work: palace rescue and federal funding use 120-day
windows, regional reconciliation uses 150 days, site extraction uses 90 days,
and the first full convergence review uses 180 days. A timeout records the
failure and exposes recovery; it never awards the terminal focus for free.

The route ledgers retain named obligations, failed bargains, fabricated
evidence, patron pressure, provincial fiscal failures, and convergence review
history through cleanup. Recovery can narrow containment, repair a broken
compact, reopen a provincial convention, disarm Rival Irelands, fulfil penance,
or separate failed convergence components. No route uses a daily or monthly
world scan, automatic core/ownership transfer, free war, free division, free
manpower/equipment, or unexplained resource deposit.

## Event families and pacing

Event descriptions explain the situation, the stake, and the system affected.
Options include a separate exact-effect line using fixed values or event-owned
preview variables. Report events use distinct registered images.

Cadence:

- dated historical events fire once in a bounded window;
- ordinary flavour dispatch runs monthly or through explicit pulses, resumes
  from a durable next-entry cursor, and advances past ineligible slots without
  consuming that pulse's single report admission.
- urgent incidents are launched by their system or mission, not a universal daily
  dispatcher;
- foreign reactions use one paced broker with a 120-day replay cooldown and at
  most one actor response per pulse, plus an optional digest;
- family conclusion reports are dispatched directly by conclusion evaluation,
  never enter the monthly flavour pool, and use one distinct 210x176 report
  image per family;
- every reusable pool tracks seen state and resets only for a named new campaign.

## Ideas, laws, offices, advisors, and companies

Every persistent institution has:

- owner route and unlock source;
- visible current effect;
- upgrade/downgrade/replace path;
- removal on incompatible government, defeat, or cleanup;
- AI use and localisation;
- icon/portrait source.

Advisor pool focuses name unlocked people. Route/date/office requirements remain
visible. Real advisors use sourced portraits and the current Chaos Redux advisor
card workflow. Industrial concerns and MIOs have selectable definitions, icons,
AI weights, and a playable material contract.

## Country identity

Route transformations update leader, party/organization, offices, cosmetic name,
flag, laws, advisors, commander availability, foreign relationships, territorial
policy, and postwar identity together. A cosmetic tag cannot be the only reward.

## Cleanup registry

Every family owns one cleanup effect that clears:

- temporary route and mission flags;
- global event targets;
- timed diplomatic windows;
- obsolete decision categories;
- incompatible advisor pools and offices;
- temporary GUI stage/frame state;
- occupation/settlement records only after their outcome is archived.

Persistent achievements and historical records are not cleared.

## Conclusion evaluation and recovery

Route evaluation and archival are separate operations. Full and partial results
dispatch the matching family report and archive only from that report's terminal
option. Blocked and failed results keep the route open, preserve the exact
blocked-surface flags, and expose a 120-day family recovery project. Recovery
clears only the repairable current blockage, retains durable failure history, and
starts a fresh 180-day review. Conclusion reports are direct-dispatch events and
never enter the ordinary flavour dispatcher.
