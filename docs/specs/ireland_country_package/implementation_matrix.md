## Acceptance scenarios

1. Historical 1936–1951: constitutional settlement, ports, neutrality, Emergency,
   shipping, demobilisation, republic and reconstruction.
2. Democratic unity: lawful Northern settlement, no conquest, integration review.
3. Congress: civilian worker institutions, supply economy, liberation policy.
4. Blueshirt: internal command split, material mobilization, ultimatum/refusal,
   bounded coercive campaign and settlement.
5. Oligarchic: producer conflict, commercial compact, protectorate/access outcome.
6. Restoration: constitutional legitimacy, provincial settlement, Dál Riata
   claim, refusal/enforcement and postwar review.
7. Failure/recovery: cabinet collapse, supply crisis, failed demand, occupied
   holding, route cleanup.
8. Hidden route: serious cultural work remains useful without reveal; impossible
   content appears only with permission and full evidence conditions.
