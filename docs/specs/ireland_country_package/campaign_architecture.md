# Ireland Country Package — Campaign Architecture

## Opening: three trunks only

The opening camera centres on the National Settlement. Three visible roots form
an unmistakable fan:

```text
                     The State Still Defined
                              |
          +-------------------+-------------------+
          |                   |                   |
 Government and Politics  National Development  Defence and Security
          |                   |                   |
 constitution/election    farms/power/transport army/air/coast/intelligence
          +-------------------+-------------------+
                              |
                       National Settlement
                              |
              five public political families
```

The first screen shows the identity of each trunk through title, icon family,
colour grouping, and straight vertical geometry. Politics and industry do not
drift horizontally across unrelated lanes. Branch navigation centres on the
middle of the chosen lane, including its revealed descendants.

## Progressive disclosure

| Reveal | Trigger | What appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| opening | game initialization | only three trunks and their first shared projects |
| constitutional debate | first Parliament stage | constitutional/election continuations and visible locked family band |
| National Settlement | settlement recorded | five public family gateways and National Question foreshadowing |
| family commitment | one gateway completed | that family’s internal profiles, cabinet, economy, security and foreign posture |
| Northern proof | Northern contacts + safeguards | territorial method stage and its exact prerequisites |
| wartime emergency | world war/incident/readiness condition | emergency supply, neutrality incidents, intelligence and mobilisation |
| cultural evidence | serious research projects | restoration/exceptional evidence lane; impossible content remains hidden |
| Atlantic policy | Northern method resolved | family-specific external policy and postwar settlement |

`allow_branch` may conceal exceptional families. Ordinary content should prefer a
visible locked card with explicit requirements so the player can understand the
campaign ahead.

## Public family band

All five gateways sit on one row directly below the Settlement and are pairwise
exclusive. The family lock and downstream profile lock remain separate.

### Democratic republican government

Profiles: de Valera constitutional continuity, Lemass developmental succession,
Fine Gael constitutional opposition, Labour parliamentary government, Clann and
inter-party coalition.

Campaign:

- establish constitutional legitimacy and cabinet confidence;
- choose protection, trade reopening, social reconstruction, or developmental
  state investment;
- professionalize security while preserving civilian control;
- defend neutrality, cooperate discreetly, or seek formal democratic security;
- pursue Northern settlement by guarantees and plebiscite;
- conclude through constitutional republic, coalition reconstruction, or a
  durable neutral democratic state.

Failure/recovery: cabinet collapse, emergency overreach, coalition fracture, and
electoral defeat open a confidence-restoration sequence rather than deleting the
shared economy/defence progress.

### Congress socialist republic

Profiles: plural workers' congress, disciplined vanguard, Labour-Congress bridge.

Campaign:

- build trade-union, tenant, smallholder, and local council support;
- settle civilian versus armed authority;
- establish cooperative procurement and worker-controlled reconstruction;
- choose international labour diplomacy or revolutionary security contacts;
- pursue an all-island workers' campaign and support neighbouring republicans;
- conclude through a civilian workers' republic, coalition social republic, or
  failed-command recovery.

Failure/recovery: armed faction dominance, strike exhaustion, clerical backlash,
and supply collapse can return power to a civilian congress or parliamentary
Labour compromise.

### Blueshirt authoritarian state

Profiles: O'Duffy personal command, national corporate chamber, emergency
directorate; Ailtirí remains a later hidden escalation rather than an ordinary
gateway.

Campaign:

- mobilize veteran networks and resolve army/Garda loyalty;
- choose personal command or corporate institutions;
- direct motorization, strategic works, censorship, and anti-communist security;
- bargain with Church, business, rural producers, and foreign sponsors;
- press the border question and, after refusal/readiness, unlock staged Atlantic
  coercion;
- conclude through a corporate state, military emergency regime, internal coup,
  or postwar demobilization.

Failure/recovery: leader incapacity, army refusal, failed ultimatum, and economic
shortage can trigger a directorate, constitutional restoration, or collapse.

### Producer-estate oligarchy

Profiles: constitutional vocationalism, creditor cabinet, industrial syndicates,
rural producer boards, executive estates.

Campaign:

- convert consultative vocational bodies into governing institutions;
- balance creditors, professions, industrial firms, farmers, labour, and Church;
- use contracts and controlled investment rather than mass-party mobilization;
- build commercial diplomacy, port access, and protectorate policy;
- settle Belfast through customs and industrial leverage;
- conclude through restricted constitutional estates, managed social peace, or
  a democratic rollback after producer conflict.

Failure/recovery: producer veto, rural-price crisis, and labour opposition may
force a broader franchise or emergency technocratic cabinet.

### Constitutional restoration

Profiles: elective ceremonial crown, guardian crown, provincial compact; sacral
or impossible variants remain hidden.

Campaign:

- turn cultural legitimacy into a public constitutional proposal;
- select a claimant procedure, household, provincial council, and legal charter;
- prove that defence and administration obey the modern state;
- settle Ulster through provincial assent;
- pursue a bounded Dál Riata claim only after the High Kingship conclusion;
- conclude through constitutional restoration, provincial federation, abdication,
  or hidden transformation.

Failure/recovery: claimant scandal, provincial refusal, army concern, and public
rejection can return to a ceremonial presidency or constitutional republic.

## Shared support lanes react to government

| Lane | Democratic | Socialist | Fascist | Oligarchic | Restoration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| agriculture | credit, export guarantees | cooperatives and tenant councils | compulsory procurement | producer boards | provincial land compact |
| industry | development authority/competition | public works and worker control | directed contracts | chartered syndicates | household/provincial patronage |
| army | professional civilian command | people's defence under civilian congress | motorized command | contracted professional force | crown under constitutional command |
| navy | neutral patrol and convoy protection | anti-blockade maritime service | Atlantic coercion | commercial access fleet | western sea mandate |
| diplomacy | neutrality/guarantees | labour republics | ultimata and sponsors | concessions/protectorates | provincial legitimacy |

Shared focuses set capability state; route-specific follow-ups convert it into a
different institution or operation. No route repeats the same project under a
different ideological title.

## Conclusions

Every public family conclusion requires:

- stable head of government and succession/office state;
- resolved family/profile authority;
- one completed economic institution;
- one completed defence model;
- a neutrality/alignment posture;
- a Northern policy or explicit decision to preserve partition;
- emergency-power settlement;
- postwar reconstruction or demobilisation;
- persistent decisions that keep the campaign playable.

Capstones that merely set a `*_complete` flag are intermediate milestones.
