## AI contract

Historical AI priorities:

1. constitutional settlement and Fianna Fáil continuity;
2. 1938 trade/Treaty Port settlement;
3. neutrality, emergency supply, coastwatch, and restrained cooperation;
4. no coercive territorial route;
5. demobilisation, constitutional republic, and postwar reconstruction.

Ahistorical AI selects one public family only after the Settlement. It then picks
one compatible profile and one economic/defence/diplomatic posture. It must not
queue mutually exclusive profiles. Hidden/extremist/absurd families have zero
weight unless the game rule or rare permission flag explicitly allows them.

Territorial AI rules:

- democratic AI never selects conquest;
- socialist AI prefers liberation and subject release;
- oligarchic AI prefers access, concessions, and protectorates;
- fascist/restoration AI may select force only after readiness, reserves, naval
  proof, diplomatic refusal, supply access, and favourable strength ratios;
- no AI launches a second demand or settlement mission while one is active;
- timeout/failure changes priorities and can select recovery.

focuses at factor `0` in `IRE_ireland_historical`. Nonhistorical plans become

## Balance budgets

The package uses centralized script constants for tuning. Exact values are tested
against cumulative route totals, not one card at a time.

### Focus reward bands

| Tier | Typical duration | Reward budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| survey/foreshadow | 35 days | information + small XP or operation preview |
| institution/project | 70 days | one building/contract/capability or named mission |
| major programme | 70 days | multi-step system unlock plus bounded material grant |
| route commitment | 35 days | exclusivity + institution/decision change, no filler bonus |
| conclusion | 70 days | identity/institution settlement and continuing gameplay |

Material grants are bounded by route totals. Free factories are reserved for
specific construction programmes with state slots and historical capacity.
Equipment delivery is a bridge to production, not a replacement army.

### Operation inputs

Operations scale with their fiction:

- industrial contracts: PP administration plus factory time/resources;
- mobilisation/exercises: equipment, manpower, fuel, command power, or XP;
- shipping: convoys, fuel, dockyard/port capacity;
- territorial enforcement: reserves, readiness, naval reach, strength, refusal;
- postwar settlement: control, compliance/resistance, administration, time.

## Focus filters

Search filters are strict direct-effect metadata:

- Army/Navy/Air XP only when that XP is granted directly;
- Research only for direct technology/doctrine/research-speed effects;
- Industry only for construction, production, company/MIO, infrastructure, or
  industrial contract effects;
- Annexation only for claims, war goals, territorial transfer, occupation, or
  integration operations;
- Manpower only for direct manpower, mobilization law, unit, or recruitment
  capability.

## Visual assets

- Real leaders/advisors use sourced historical photographs with source/rights
  records. Advisor cards use the current Chaos Redux canonical workflow.
- Historical flags and attested symbols use sourced evidence.
- Generated art is reserved for alternate, symbolic, hidden, supernatural, or
  impossible subjects.
- Every focus has a distinct readable 94x86 icon and shine registration; repeated
  generic seals are not acceptable for route gateways.
- Every report event has a registered 210x176 image that identifies its event
  family. No missing or unrelated fallback report image.
- Parliament enabled buttons use green/brass; unavailable buttons are visibly
  greyed by engine state. Seat diagrams and text remain aligned at 1920 and 2560.

The 38 exceptional focus consumers use distinct `GFX_focus_ireland_<id>`
registrations and matching one-frame shine registrations in
`interface/ireland_icons.gfx` and `interface/ireland_focus_shine.gfx`. Their
final 94x86 DDS files, source PNGs, processed previews, and manifest are recorded
under `docs/assets/ireland_exceptional_routes_depth_2026-08-14/`. No exceptional
focus reuses another focus's sprite.

Every new focus, reward tooltip, and description has an English key in
`localisation/english/ireland_l_english.yml` encoded with UTF-8 BOM. Otherworld
copy distinguishes local courts, literary associations, and human interpreters
and does not present folklore as twentieth-century institutional evidence.

## Localisation direction

Focus descriptions answer: what problem is being solved, who supports/opposes it,
and what policy is chosen. Reward tooltips name concrete outputs and operation
unlocks. Decision/event tooltips state exact visible effects and lifecycle.

No player text says content was added, reworked, capped, or patched. Serious
violence, repression, antisemitism, sectarianism, occupation, and suffering use
specific sober language and show consequences.

## Achievements

Achievements verify campaign mastery, not focus clicks. Candidate families:

- preserve neutrality while maintaining high readiness and shipping;
- lawful all-Ireland settlement with low resistance;
- inter-party republic and full postwar reconstruction;
- Congress civilian republic without military dominance;
- corporate state surviving a producer crisis;
- Dál Riata settlement after foreign refusal and resolved occupation;
- hidden cultural conclusion with all provenance obligations;
- no automatic cores or free-transfer triggers.

Every achievement has route, difficulty, failure/disqualifier, and exact trigger
ownership before implementation.

## Postwar family AI and presentation

Once its capstone and shared postwar foundation are complete, AI treats its own
family settlement as critical. Blocked AI prioritises recovery before optional
postwar work and waits for a free civilian factory. Historical AI remains
non-coercive. Five family reports use distinct 210x176 art, and the ten settlement
and recovery actions use distinct decision icons; no generic or unrelated art is
an acceptable substitute.
