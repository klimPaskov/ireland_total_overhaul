# Ireland value-purpose matrix

This matrix defines the values the player is expected to understand. “Consumer” means a real gameplay gate, result, or action family. The GUI tooltip should name representative player-facing consumers rather than script identifiers.

## State

| Value | Purpose | Representative consumers | Improvement sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National Settlement | Measures whether institutions accept a common constitutional settlement | route openings, constitutional reviews, conclusions, crisis outcomes | parliamentary programme, lawful settlements, recorded compromises |
| Social Consent | Measures public tolerance for the government's programme and burdens | public-order choices, mobilisation, cultural and Northern campaigns | negotiated policy, representation, protected services |
| Neutrality Integrity | Measures whether equal external rules can be enforced | neutrality incidents, foreign access, security and conclusion gates | consistent rules, documented enforcement, incident resolution |
| Administrative Reach | Measures whether the state can execute policy outside cabinet declarations | territorial instruments, integration, programme delivery | local government, transport, professional administration |
| Dáil Mandate | Measures reliable parliamentary authority beyond raw seats | parliamentary actions, route commitments, coercive and postwar gates | elections, confidence, negotiated support |

## Provision

National Provision is the lowest effective component, not an average. The hub must say which component limits it.

| Value | Purpose | Representative consumers | Improvement sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Food | Sustains civilians, mobilisation and relief | provision projects, crises, exceptional commitments | tillage, sugar, cooperatives, rural credit |
| Energy | Sustains industry, transport and military preparation | factories, fuel projects, readiness and crisis response | ESB, turf, fuel reserve, power works |
| Transport | Moves food, equipment and administration | supply, territorial administration, industrial contracts | roads, railways, ports and state transport |
| Maritime Access | Keeps imports and coastal supply open | convoy decisions, Marine Service, emergency provision | shipping registry, convoys, ports and escorts |

## Readiness

National Readiness is the lowest effective component. A large army does not compensate for a missing command, supply, warning, or intelligence system.

| Value | Purpose | Representative consumers | Improvement sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command | Coherent civilian and military authority | mobilisation and coercive missions | command audit, staff, civilian oversight |
| Equipment | Material capacity to execute plans | territorial and exceptional missions | deliveries, contracts, factories and reserves |
| Mobilisation | Ability to call and sustain personnel | emergency and coercive missions | reserve planning, public authority, training |
| Supply | Ability to support deployed forces | border, Atlantic and regional operations | transport, depots, logistics |
| Warning | Time to react to air and maritime incidents | neutrality incidents, air defence | observers, radar, coastwatch |
| Maritime Protection | Ability to secure ports, convoys and approaches | Marine Service, Atlantic actions | patrols, naval staff, convoy office |
| Intelligence | Evidence and security reach | counter-subversion, exceptional proof, territorial planning | intelligence service, archives, liaison |

## Values intentionally not shown

The following classes remain internal: event option preview deltas, event dispatch cursors, cached AI profile values, dirty counters, temporary clamp variables, mission-capacity accounting, frame indices, selection variables, and one-use helper flags. They may be documented for maintainers, but presenting them as national mechanics would increase confusion without giving the player agency.
