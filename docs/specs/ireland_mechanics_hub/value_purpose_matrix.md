# Cabinet Briefing public-state matrix

This is the complete player-facing set. Internal component values remain authoritative in their native systems but are not promoted into permanent dashboard records.

| Card | Public form | Gameplay significance | Existing improvement route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government | Qualitative: Steady, Contested, Strained, or Settled | Whether the parliamentary programme and governing authority can sustain the national course | Parliament actions and their required constitutional focuses |
| National Supply | One Provision aggregate | Civil capacity for industry, relief, and mobilisation | Provision focuses and existing contract decisions |
| Defence | One Readiness aggregate | Ability to sustain emergency, service, and territorial commitments | Army, air, naval, warning, supply, and intelligence focuses and decisions |
| National Question | Qualitative unopened/recovery/settled state or current settlement stage | Progress and legality of Northern settlement and integration | Northern Settlement Ledger, plebiscite/compact/recovery, and territorial decisions |

Not displayed as permanent cards: bilateral leverage, Foreign Exposure, Sponsor Dependence, individual Provision/Readiness components, cultural records, evidence counts, exceptional obligations, event-option preview deltas, dispatcher state, or technical counters.
