# Cabinet Briefing asset contract

The compact briefing reuses completed Ireland assets:

- `gfx/interface/ireland/gui/conditions/focus_inlay_background.dds` for the cabinet paper;
- four distinct registered decision-category emblems for Government, Provision, Readiness, and Northern policy;
- the Parliament step button for the single contextual action;
- the registered National Situation decision-category icon.

The visual hierarchy is paper first, four distinct emblems second, compact colour-coded state text third, and one optional action last. No extra status badges sit over the emblems.

`tab_selected.dds` and `row_selected.dds` belong to the rejected diagnostic-ledger presentation. Keep their source records as historical asset evidence, but do not wire them into the Cabinet Briefing.

No replacement art, placeholder, animated ornament, or additional category emblem is required.
