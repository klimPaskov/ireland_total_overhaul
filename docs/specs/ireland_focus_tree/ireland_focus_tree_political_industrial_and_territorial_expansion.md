# Ireland public politics, industry, and territorial-policy specification

Status: canonical source specification.

This specification owns the implemented public political-family expansion,
advisor and industrial-company unlock packages, territorial-policy lane, and
their cross-surface acceptance contract. Where a broad earlier specification
uses working labels, the exact identifiers below take precedence.

## Public political families

The 1936 opening exposes only the three shared trunks for government, national
development, and defence. Completing the National Settlement reveals five
plain-language, mutually exclusive public political gateways:

| Family | Gateway | Governing identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| democratic | `ireland_republican_government` | constitutional parliamentary government |
| communist | `ireland_congress_reform_bridge` | workers' republic through the Republican Congress |
| fascist | `ireland_oduffy_network_revival` | O'Duffy's national corporate state |
| oligarchic | `ireland_vocational_constitution` | restricted producer-estate government |
| restoration | `ireland_high_king_evidence_gate` | speculative constitutional High Kingship |

The gateways use `ireland_political_family_committed` and one durable
`ireland_family_*` flag. They do not consume the downstream profile lock.
Every family provides at least sixteen substantive family focuses, with at
least eight selectable in a normal family playthrough, and covers governing
form, economic institution, security, foreign posture, advisors, company
interaction, Northern policy, reconstruction or recovery, and a conclusion.
The five gateways form one readable post-Settlement fan. Family depth cannot
be satisfied by marker-only or generic-reward leaves.

## Advisors and industrial capabilities

Five cabinet focuses open constitutional, Congress, Blueshirt,
producer-estate, and restoration advisor pools. The democratic constitutional
service pool must be reachable from every democratic profile. Tooltips name
every advisor whose pool is opened and any route, date, or office condition
that still remains.

The company focus matrix owns nine selectable capabilities and nine separate
material contracts. ESB, Irish Sugar, Turf Development Board, Great Southern
Railways, Aer Lingus, Irish Shipping, and Irish Steel are industrial concerns.
Ford Cork is a motorised/mechanised military industrial organisation. Harland
and Wolff Belfast is a naval military industrial organisation available only
after the lawful Northern settlement gate. Irish Shipping requires either the
historical March 1941 founding date or an explicit wartime emergency-founding
condition. Company names are historical to the represented period; the steel
company is Irish Steel, not the later Steel Holdings label.

## Territorial policy

`ireland_territorial_policy` becomes visible through
`ireland_define_the_national_question`. State scope is fixed:

- State 119: Northern Ireland and the five family-specific settlement methods.
- State 120: Scottish Highlands, the only restoration claim and first fascist
  external claim.
- State 122: Wales, communist liberation or later fascist escalation.
- State 123: Cornwall, later fascist escalation only.
- SCO and WLS: voluntary access, liberation, or protectorate outcomes where
  the chosen family permits them.

No external state receives an automatic core. Coercive routes require peace,
the shared readiness conclusion, Dublin, a Dáil majority, centralized
material reserves, valid live target control and faction relations, relative
strength, and actual naval reach where the target requires it. A demand starts
a target-country response chain with acceptance, refusal, and counteroffer or
partial settlement. Force becomes available only after recorded refusal.
Every saved target and temporary demand state has deterministic cleanup.

Postwar settlement is recorded per relevant holding and per SCO/WLS subject
relationship. Treating one holding cannot close the review while another
controlled holding or eligible subject remains unresolved. Local-rights,
protectorate/release, and Highlands integration-review outcomes each write
their own record. The Atlantic conclusion and its mission close only when the
shared per-holding trigger proves that every currently retained relationship
has an outcome. Mission capacity and temporary reservation state are updated
on start, loss, cancellation, timeout, conclusion, and route cleanup.

## Parliament availability

The four National Settlement controls use shared guarded executors and live
red/green requirement rows. Costs are 0/25/25/35 Political Power for Dáil,
Seanad, confidence, and joint budget. Grey means at least one displayed stage,
focus, Dublin, majority, completion, or affordability condition is false.

## Acceptance

Completion requires all of the following:

- the focus graph retains only three opening trunks and has no crossings,
  connector-through-node intersections, long connectors, or spacing defects;
- the five public families satisfy the depth and policy-domain contract;
- all advisor, concern, MIO, contract, decision, event, AI, localisation, and
  sprite references resolve without fallback assets;
- territorial diplomacy is target-scoped and postwar settlement is
  per-holding, capacity-safe, and cleanup-complete;
- the Chaos Redux advisor-card package passes its measured-template and
  independent visual gates; and
- active plans and completion reports cite current source and audit evidence.
