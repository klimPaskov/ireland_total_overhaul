# Ireland exceptional-route lifecycle

The High Kingship, Five Provinces, and Otherworld routes use existing decision
categories. They add no fourth category, periodic world scan, state transfer,
core, war goal, free formation, stockpile, or resource deposit.

`ireland_refresh_exceptional_route_state` is called from each exceptional
focus, event option, decision, and mission result. It initialises the ledger,
reconciles lawful provincial participation, retains profile selection, and
replaces only the existing governing/society/security spirit trio.

Each exceptional mission is registered with the existing global queue and its
host category's counter. Crown, Rival Irelands, and convergence reviews retain
the route-review reservation; routine obligations cannot displace them.

High Kingship keeps Royal Legitimacy, Household Reach, and Royal Obligation
Burden. Captivity permits two household audits before the 120-day break mission;
sacral rule uses named geasa and a 120-day circuit. Neither recovery awards a
terminal focus.

Five Provinces records consent through provincial mandates, Federal Cohesion,
and Middle Balance. An Ulster mandate only follows a lawful-access record. The
Empty Chair has no claim, core, ownership, or invented mandate.

Otherworld obligations are named due flags backed by material or political
commitments. The Fian mission certifies a player-built formation only when it
is deployed in Dublin, Cork, or Donegal at 90% strength and 80% organisation,
then commits 500 manpower, 500 infantry equipment, 20 support equipment, and
20 command power. It creates neither a unit nor a stockpile. Extraction creates
restitution pressure; it never awards a deposit or stockpile.

The 180-day Three Orders review preserves valid component histories on failure,
clears stale active missions, and reopens the relevant recovery work.

## Presentation assets

Every exceptional action and mission has a distinct 32x32 DDS sprite, and events
`438–460` have distinct 210x176 report cards. The ten cosmetic identities have
normal, medium, and small uncompressed 32-bit TGA flag files:
`IRE_IRELAND_CAPTIVE_THRONE`, `IRE_IRELAND_CONFEDERATION`,
`IRE_IRELAND_FIFTH_PROVINCE`, `IRE_IRELAND_RIVAL_PROVINCES`,
`IRE_IRELAND_TUATHA_SOVEREIGNTY`, `IRE_IRELAND_FIANNA_GUARDIANSHIP`,
`IRE_IRELAND_SEALED_STATE`, `IRE_IRELAND_CROWN_AND_PROVINCES`,
`IRE_IRELAND_HIGH_KING_TWO_IRELANDS`, and
`IRE_IRELAND_FIVE_COURTS_OTHERWORLD`.

The source PNGs, processed previews, final-asset inventory, prompts, contact sheets, and
copy-ready wiring are recorded under
`docs/assets/ireland_exceptional_routes_depth_2026-08-14/`. No presentation
surface uses a placeholder or unrelated fallback.

## Future depth

Future work may add further obligations only when each remains an active,
bounded player commitment with its own resolution and cleanup. It must not make
obligations passive or add a global periodic scan.
