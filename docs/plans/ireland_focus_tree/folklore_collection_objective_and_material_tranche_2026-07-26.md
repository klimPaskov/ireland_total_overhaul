Feature slug: `ireland_focus_tree`

## Scope

This tranche binds the serious-culture folklore mission to its canonical institutional groundwork and gives the collector operation concrete physical inputs. It does not claim live engine execution, event firing, AI behavior, asset clearance, or scenario completion.

## Canonical work preserved

`Expand Folklore Collection` remains an H-level Irish Folklore Commission and schools-network action. It now requires the serious-culture/archive lane, a school or language-service record, archival groundwork, historic-site custody, Connacht control, 250 manpower, and 5 Trucks before launching `Collect A National Folklore Cycle`.

The mission map objective retains Connacht infrastructure and a concealed-evidence region, and now also requires its active action and mission plus the same school, archive, and custody proofs. The dated 180-day mission still owns full, partial, failure, escalation, provenance, and collector cleanup outcomes.
