# Ireland exclusive route-profile and spirit tranche — 2026-07-29

## Scope

This tranche closes the durable-profile coexistence defect and the canonical
route-spirit compression identified by the independent feature-completion
audit. It does not alter the 30-profile roster, add a fourth persistent spirit
family, change the 10 temporary AI overlays, or promote an uncleared source
asset.

## Durable profile authority

`ireland_sync_route_profile` now clears the complete profile registry and
selects one profile through a single ordered `if`/`else_if` chain.

The precedence is:

1. reviewed convergence merger;
2. unresolved convergence review using the Otherworld compact as host;
3. exact Otherworld, Five Provinces, and High Kingship subprofiles;
4. constitutional restoration and exact radical/corporate subprofiles;
5. constitutional route profiles;
6. serious-culture and pre-commitment AI profiles;
7. historical de Valera fallback.

The component High Kingship, Five Provinces, and Otherworld flags remain
historical evidence during convergence. They no longer create simultaneous AI
profiles. `ireland_profile_convergence_order` is written only after
`ireland_convergence_order_concluded`; the capstone alone retains
`ireland_profile_otherworld_compact` throughout the 180-day review.

## Persistent spirit forms

Ten missing mutually exclusive trio states were added:

- historical de Valera stewardship;
- IRA GHQ command;
- Congress vanguard;
- institutional O'Duffy corporate order;
- producer-council state;
- sacral High Kingship;
- Five Provinces pentarchy;
- Otherworld dominion;
- Otherworld containment;
- reviewed convergence order.

Each state has one Governing Order idea, one Organised Society idea, and one
Security and External Position idea. The spirit refresh uses the exclusive
durable profile rather than independent route flags, so one refresh installs
one trio. Existing corruption, collapse, patronage, broken-services,
compromised-security, and disarmed-position overrides remove the new slot forms
before installing their failure replacement.
