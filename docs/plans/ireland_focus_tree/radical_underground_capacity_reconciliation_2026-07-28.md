## Evidence

of two and reserved one organisational mission plus one seizure or constituent
mission. The historical map version did not yet list those two mission rows,
while its fixed inventory still totalled 54.

The historical source snapshot preserved the fixed 54-family inventory while
keeping the category action-only. That snapshot has been replaced by the M2
merge and ordinal reassignment.

## Disposition

M2 is the accepted disposition. The by-election mode is carried by
`Conduct a Free General Election`, the parish-audit mode is carried by `Keep
Essential Services Running`, and ordinals 006 and 009 are now the physically
nested Radical/Underground missions `Consolidate the Movement Network` and
`Secure Constituent Authority`. The exact 13/140/54 inventory is preserved.
Both families have named map objectives, mode-specific thresholds, full and
partial success, failure, escalation, cancellation, AI handling, and cleanup.

Live capacity and AI scenarios remain outside this document's historical
analysis, but the source-level authority question is settled by
