## Delivered systems

- The runtime tree contains 215 manually reviewed focuses and 222 connectors.
- The opening remains limited to government, national development, and
  defence. The National Settlement then reveals five plain-language,
  mutually-exclusive families: parliamentary democracy, a workers' republic,
  a fascist corporate state, producer-estate oligarchy, and constitutional

### Industrial concerns and advisors

Nine industrial capabilities are defined and wired: the selectable Electricity
Supply Board, Irish Sugar, Turf Development Board, Great Southern Railways,
Aer Lingus, Irish Shipping, and Irish Steel concerns, plus the Ford Cork and
settlement-gated Harland and Wolff military industrial organisations. Each has
one separate 25-Political-Power, 35-day, one-use
contract producing infrastructure, a factory, equipment, fuel, convoys, or
experience. No company shares or consumes another company's one-shot action.

The five cabinet focuses expose constitutional, Congress, Blueshirt,
professional-estate, and restoration advisor pools. Existing date, office,
vacancy, leadership, and route restrictions remain in force. All 45 runtime
advisor cards were regenerated with the current Chaos Redux measured-template
workflow and independently approved. Each is a 65x67 uncompressed BGRA DDS
with native, 4x, placement, 8x alignment, metadata, and source evidence.

### Territorial policy

The territorial category owns:

- five political-family methods for the Northern question;
- democratic and oligarchic access diplomacy;
- communist Welsh and Scottish republican support;
- fascist State 119 enforcement and staged State 120, 122, and 123 claims;
- a restoration claim on State 120 only; and
- local-rights, Scottish/Welsh protectorate, Highlands integration-review,
  and 365-day postwar settlement procedures.

Coercive preparations cost 50 Political Power and 70 days. They require peace,
the readiness conclusion, Dublin, a Dáil majority, at least 5,000 manpower,
1,000 infantry equipment, 100 support equipment, 20 convoys, 1,000 fuel, 50
Command Power, and a target controller below the exact strength threshold.
The complete effect repeats the full readiness, live-controller, faction, and
strength checks. A changed target or strategic condition returns 25 Political
Power instead of creating an invalid goal. Timed war goals expire after 180
days. No external state receives an automatic core.

The Atlantic settlement mission uses the shared global and postwar mission
capacity system. Its active marker is cleared on conclusion, timeout, route
cleanup, or loss of every relevant controlled-state and subject relationship.
