## Rights findings applied

- The exact UCD scans are provenance-traced, but the UCD presentation does not
  state an image-level public-domain, CC0, CC-BY, or derivative-use licence.
  DIFP is textual provenance, not an image licence.
- Military Archives identifies the exact EDP and ADC catalogue leads, but the
  local pages are finding aids rather than scans of the underlying objects.
  Military Archives states that online access copies are its copyright and
  directs reproduction requests to the Duty Archivist; written permission is
  required for publication.
- The exact Blacksod sheet is available from Met Eireann, but its page and
  copyright notice do not grant image-level derivative or redistribution
  permission. The D-Day Overlord copy cannot license the underlying Met
  Eireann object.
- The Wikimedia Commons scan of the Irish Emergency Powers Order 1939 has a
  public-domain statement, and the Northern Ireland 1940 statutory order has
  an OGL v3 statement. Both are real, image-level reusable documents, but
  neither is the assigned UCD correspondence, EDP plan, ADC object, or
  Blacksod sheet, so neither is a valid row promotion.
- The Military Archives Flickr aerial-photo project states non-commercial
  sharing terms, but its aerial/lighthouse images are not the assigned EDP or
  ADC objects, and the archive's general reproduction terms require
  confirmation before extending that statement to other holdings.
