## Clearance interpretation

The current manifest contains 35 C1 provenance leads, 26 C2
restricted/review-pending records, 82 C3 conditional records, and 26 C4
records. It records 48 complete outputs, 64 review-gated packages, and 57
blocked rows. The official state-harp package is C4 for the bounded
state-identity reference use; the older Dalway-harp candidate remains a
research-only comparison. S-16, S-19, S-39, and S-40 are the complete
symbol/document runtime packages. S-19 uses the exact public-domain 1903 *An
Claidheamh Soluis* object and is not identified as the controlled NLI 1915
handbill. Thirty-four C3 portraits remain review-gated even though candidate
derivatives are present. P-33 John Hayes and P-51 Seán Murray have exact
conditional review sources preserved under `docs/portraits/`. P-35 Maurice
Twomey is the C3-complete portrait with Berkeley/Bancroft attribution
conditions, and P-59 J. J. Walsh is the C4-complete National Library of
Ireland/Wikimedia Commons portrait. The manifest status remains authoritative;
no clearance is inferred merely from a processed or staging DDS file.

Catalogue discovery and secondary biographies were used to identify leads, not to infer permission. Modern reenactments, film stills, tourism images, AI reconstructions, invented historical marks, untraceable mirrors, and wrong-era material were rejected.
