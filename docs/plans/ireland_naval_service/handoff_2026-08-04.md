# Ireland maritime-service implementation handoff

## Implemented surfaces

- Focus branch: `common/national_focus/ireland_focus_tree.txt`
  - ten compact, tagged naval focuses from Marine Service reconstruction through
    the regional maritime-defence pact;
  - the reserve focus only unlocks the formation decision, preventing a second
    free Marine Service Company from being created by the focus and decision
    together;
  - the Atlantic Convoy Office now uses one prerequisite block containing both
    naval lanes.
- Decision category and actions:
  - `common/decisions/categories/ireland_categories.txt`
  - `common/decisions/ireland_marine_service.txt`
  - four staged actions with visible political-power, command-power, convoy,
    fuel, manpower, infantry-equipment, and support-equipment costs.
- Historical military detail:
  - `history/units/IRE_1936.txt`
  - `history/units/IRE_1939.txt`
  - coastal defence, Marine Service, coastal watch, and emergency reserve
    templates; starting OOB remains the single Irish Army High Command.
- Name pools:
  - `common/units/names_divisions/IRE_ireland_total_overhaul.txt`
  - `common/units/names_ships/IRE_ireland_total_overhaul.txt`
  - fixed historical formation names use `unordered`; ship pools cover patrol,
    training, reserve patrol/rescue, light cruiser, and submarine services.
- Intelligence agency:
  - `common/intelligence_agencies/ireland_total_overhaul.txt`
  - `interface/ireland_agencies.gfx`
  - two-frame 234x119 Ireland agency logo with G2, Coastwatch, and neutral
    shipping names.
- Icons and presentation:
  - naval focus aliases and decision/category sprites are registered in
    `interface/ireland_icons.gfx`;
  - BoP side icons are registered in `interface/ireland_bop_assets.gfx`;
  - runtime idea sprites are in `interface/ireland_secondary_assets.gfx`;
  - report/news sprites are registered in `interface/ireland_event_art.gfx`
    and `interface/ireland_sourced_assets.gfx`.
- Advisor workflow:
  - the Chaos Redux template and source-derived script are copied to
    `assets/ireland_focus_tree/advisor_workflow/`;
  - the script has only a Pillow compatibility change (`getdata()` instead of
    `get_flattened_data()`); all 45 Ireland advisor cards were regenerated.

## Historical interpretation

Ireland did not operate a pre-war fleet comparable to the major naval powers.
The branch therefore models a plausible expansion of coastal patrol,
harbour-control, rescue, convoy, and Marine Service institutions. The
starting OOB does not contain a free navy or free marine formation; naval units
and the intelligence agency are unlocked through the branch and its costs.
The cruiser and submarine name groups support an alternate industrial outcome,
but no ships are spawned by the start-date history files.
