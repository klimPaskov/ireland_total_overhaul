# Atlantic prerequisite and capability-payoff implementation

Date: 2026-08-14

The Atlantic conclusion now uses one prerequisite block containing the rights,
protectorate, and integration policy focuses. This gives the block its intended
OR semantics while the per-holding resolution trigger remains the authoritative
completion test.

The capability-payoff pass removes unrelated stability and free support
equipment from the Atlantic security, neighbouring-republic, concessions,
postwar-office, rights-charter, and final-settlement focuses. Their reward
tooltips now name the decisions, operations, policies, and accounting outcomes
they actually unlock. Stability filters were removed where the focus no longer
has a direct stability effect.

Focus inspection after the change reports 218 focuses and 226 connectors with no
crossings, node intersections, long connectors, or spacing defects. The remaining
clerical two-column detour is owned by the separate family-band layout pass.
