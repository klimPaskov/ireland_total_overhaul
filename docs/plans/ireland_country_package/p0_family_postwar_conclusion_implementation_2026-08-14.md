# P0 family postwar conclusion implementation

Date: 2026-08-14

Implemented the accepted family-conclusion architecture:

- five 180-day family institutional settlement projects;
- five 120-day family recovery projects;
- centralized postwar foundation, evaluation, recovery, and archival helpers;
- five directly dispatched family reports with full, partial, and recovery
  outcomes;
- archive only from a successful or partial report option;
- durable failure history plus repairable current blockers;
- an explicit alternate diplomatic-order project that exists only when the UN
  admission authority is unavailable;
- eleven distinct decision icons and five distinct report images.

The final conclusion review is repeatable after recovery. No whole-world pulse
or flavour dispatcher owns these reports. No project grants free factories,
equipment, cores, stability, or territorial transfers.

The remaining P0 surface is the complete renderer-backed five-family focus-band
layout pass. P1 territorial recovery and final AI prioritisation remain queued
behind that layout result.
