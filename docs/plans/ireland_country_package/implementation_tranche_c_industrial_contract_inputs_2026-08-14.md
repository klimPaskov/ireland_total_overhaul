# Industrial contract input tranche

Status: implemented on 2026-08-14.

The nine company contract decisions retain their distinct material outcomes but no longer operate as uniform Political Power purchases. Every contract reserves productive capacity for its full 35-day duration:

| Contract class | Factory reservation | Outcomes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| grid works | 2 Civilian Factories | Infrastructure |
| road and rail fleet | 1 Civilian Factory | Trucks and a Train |
| merchant tonnage | 1 Civilian Factory | Convoys |
| sugar/materials plant | 2 Civilian Factories | Civilian Factory |
| Ford motor works | 1 Civilian Factory | Trucks and Army Experience |
| Aer Lingus conversion | 1 Civilian Factory | Transport Planes and Air Experience |
| turf reserve | 1 Civilian Factory | Fuel |
| steel foundry | 2 Civilian Factories | Military Factory |
| Belfast refit | 2 Civilian Factories | Dockyard and Navy Experience |

Each decision checks that the corresponding factory capacity is free before it can begin. The active decision modifier then reserves that capacity until completion. This follows the vanilla timed-project decision pattern and keeps the contracts useful without making them free construction or a Political Power shop.

The decision descriptions state Political Power, duration, factory reservation, and completion output explicitly.
