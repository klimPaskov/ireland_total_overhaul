# Ireland territorial diplomacy report-art prompts

All five sources were generated with the built-in `$imagegen` workflow as original fictional alternate-history press photographs. Each prompt requested 1936–1945 photographic technology, period clothing and vehicles, documentary realism, no readable text, no flags/logos, no modern props, and no fake card border. The repository report-card treatment was applied locally afterward.

## `report_event_ireland_ulster_ultimatum`

1938 Irish government delegation delivering a formal territorial ultimatum at a windswept Northern Irish border bridge; Irish envoy in dark overcoat and peaked cap faces a small British/Ulster official delegation beside a stone bridge and watch post; wet Atlantic day, period staff car, tense human exchange, documentary black-and-white press photograph.

## `report_event_ireland_belfast_compact`

1939 Belfast commercial office: Irish trade envoy and Northern Irish industrial representative conclude a guarded commercial and military-access compact over an oak desk with blank folders and ledger books; Belfast harbour cranes and shipyard hull visible through rainy windows; restrained handshake, pragmatic cooperation, period black-and-white press photograph.

## `report_event_ireland_highlands_claim`

1937 remote Scottish Highland road: Irish envoy and Highland estate official inspect a disputed mountain pass; heather slopes, stone wall, croft, mist, and a 1930s utility car; two small figures and the rugged terrain read clearly; solemn exploratory claim, period black-and-white field-documentary photograph.

## `report_event_ireland_highlands_negotiation`

1938 Scottish Highlands negotiation inside a bare stone hall: Irish envoy, Highland community elder, and British official around a rough table with blank folder and enamel mug; stove and rain-blurred mullioned window; wary controlled diplomacy, period black-and-white newspaper photograph.

## `report_event_ireland_ulster_enforcement`

1940 aftermath of a refused Irish demand at a Northern Irish border crossing: Irish infantry in greatcoats and steel helmets stand behind a wet stone checkpoint while a British/Ulster officer keeps the barrier lowered; period lorry and staff car, rain, restrained menace and imminent enforcement, no combat; black-and-white war-correspondent photograph.
