Every accepted package must retain the exact `required_credit`,
`license_or_permission`, source URL, holding institution, object identifier,
and uncertainty recorded in the manifest. Acceptance does not convert C3 into
C4 and does not permit reuse outside the recorded single-person portrait
surface.

## Material condition groups

- P-43 is CC BY 2.0 and requires attribution. It is a historical
  colourisation derivative; it must be described as such.
- P-13, P-14, P-30, P-36, and P-37 rely on National Library,
  Flickr Commons, or university-source no-known-restrictions/public-domain
  records. Their institutional credits and derivative-source notes remain
  mandatory.
- P-20 is the exact trade-unionist William O'Brien in the August 1918 A. H.
  Poole Studio/National Library of Ireland object `vtls000593300`. Acceptance
  must preserve the parent-group provenance, NLI object identifier, A. H.
  Poole attribution, and the recorded Commons public-domain basis. It must not
  be confused with the Home Rule politician of the same name.
- P-05, P-06, P-09, P-17, P-18, P-19, P-21, P-22, P-23, P-25, P-27, P-28,
  P-29, P-34, P-40, P-42, P-44, P-46, P-47, P-49, P-52, P-53, P-56, P-58,
  and P-60 rely on recorded Commons public-domain, anonymous-author,
  no-known-restrictions, press-source, or jurisdiction-specific bases with the
  exact caveats in the manifest.
- P-42 has the strongest unresolved warning in the current review set:
  Commons asserts PD-Old but displays the missing-SDC-status warning, while the
  underlying Irish Police/BBC object lacks an independently verified archive
  identifier and United States status. It remains staging-only unless that
  condition is explicitly accepted.
- P-33 uses an exact named circa-1940s single-person portrait from Wikimedia
  Commons. The uploader-derived Irish public-domain claim does not establish
  the original photographer, first lawful publication, or United States
  status. It remains staging-only unless those conditions are accepted.
- P-51 uses an exact 25 April 1934 Library of Congress/NDNP newspaper page
  object. The embedded photograph has no separately identified photographer or
  rights record, and the issue falls inside the Library's warning for newspaper
  material under 95 years old. It remains staging-only unless that condition is
  explicitly accepted.

## Runtime boundary

No row in this packet is registered or promoted merely because a processed
portrait exists. Final leader DDS files belong under
`gfx/leaders/ireland/`; source originals remain preserved under
`docs/portraits/`; temporary processing and review DDS files remain under
`assets/`.

An acceptance decision should identify either:

1. all 34 rows as accepted under their individual recorded conditions;
2. a precise subset of accepted IDs; or
3. rejected IDs that must remain outside runtime while object-level
   alternatives are researched.
