# Culture, diplomacy, Northern policy, and the Naval Service reveal later as subordinate families.
# Hidden revolutionary, corporate, restoration, and Otherworld routes retain their authored gates.
# Route class legend: H = documented historical route; P = plausible constitutional or institutional route;
# A = deliberate absurdity, concealed behind independent evidence, state-interface failure and rare conditions.
