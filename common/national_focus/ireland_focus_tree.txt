# Ireland Total Overhaul national focus tree
# Overview: three opening trunks cover government and politics, national development, and defence.
# Culture, diplomacy, Northern policy, and the Naval Service reveal later as subordinate families.
# Five political families become visible after the National Settlement; only the
# supernatural and deliberately extreme escalations retain concealed evidence gates.
# Route class legend: H = documented historical route; P = plausible constitutional or institutional route;
# A = deliberate absurdity, concealed behind independent evidence, state-interface failure and rare conditions.
# Focuses set route/proof flags and hand off lifecycle work to the parent scripted systems. Every focus owns
# a specific visible reward; provision, readiness, and naval projects also deliver bounded physical results.
# Territorial focuses expose lawful, revolutionary, coercive, commercial, and
# restoration instruments. Claims and timed war-goal decisions never grant free cores.
# Route capstones call the conclusion-review helper only after material, readiness, foreign, Northern and
# postwar proof flags are present. Recovery bridges call cleanup so failed routes can be re-entered lawfully.
